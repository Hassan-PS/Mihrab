The Log tells the truth about what you entered.

• The graph is coloured by status, not by how many boxes you ticked. On time counts fully, late and make-up less, missed nothing — and a day recorded as missed is marked, not left blank.
• Open any past day: the arrows by the date, or tap its square.
• The graph reaches your first logged day and scrolls back.
• A prayer whose time has not come cannot be logged, and "Mark all on time" respects that.
• Optional: show the graph on the Today screen.
