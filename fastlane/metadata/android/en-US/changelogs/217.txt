Mihrab is now a full iPad app — and runs on Apple Silicon Macs. The layout adapts to the window: a two-column Home dashboard on wide screens, centred reading, and reflowing tools, Settings and month schedule; back to a single column when narrow. Dual-page Quran (mushaf) in landscape. New tafsir selector in Settings that stays in sync with the Quran page — and your tafsir choice now sticks instead of resetting. Smoother day carousel on large screens.
