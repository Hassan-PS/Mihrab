• A bookmark can follow your reading, so it stays where you stopped — or stay pinned to its ayah. Each bookmark has its own switch.
• Khatmah progress is the pages you actually read. Mark a page read or unread beside the surah name; pages you skipped are counted and can be gone back to.
• A dot beside the surah name says which of your places is recording.
• New: start the day at sunset, so an evening's reading counts towards the day that has begun (Settings → Quran).
