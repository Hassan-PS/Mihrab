The Log, made to work the way you were already using it.

• The notification's "Log prayer" button now actually records the prayer — including with the app closed, and on the day the alert was for.
• Swipe the day panel sideways to move between days; the arrows still work.
• Fill in earlier days, or the past three months. Both ask first and never touch a day you have already logged.
• A day with a missed prayer is marked on the graph, so a prayer you plan to make up can be found again.
• The evening prompt stops asking about a day you have already finished logging.
• The graph scrolls on the first try, and on the Today screen at all.
